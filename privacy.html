Privacy Policy

This site is Samir Sen’s personal portfolio. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and has no accounts. This page lists every way your data can leave your browser while you visit.

Draft for review. This policy describes how the site works today. Samir has not reviewed it yet, and points marked To confirm need his decision before it is final.

Who is responsible

The site is run by Samir Sen, an individual based in Kathmandu, Nepal. For any privacy question, write to samirsen66@gmail.com.

What the site itself collects

Nothing. The site has no server code, database, sign-up, cookies, analytics or tracking pixels, and it does not use your browser’s local storage. The robots, posters and project pages are static files.

Opening a case study adds ?case= to the address in your browser so the link can be shared. That change stays in your browser.

Services your browser contacts

To show the page, your browser requests files from three services. Each one receives your IP address and standard request details, such as your browser type and the page you came from, and handles them under its own policy.

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. GitHub states that when a GitHub Pages site is visited, the visitor’s IP address is logged and stored for security purposes. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces (Schibsted Grotesk and Martian Mono). Google says the Fonts API sets no cookies and that it does not use this information to build profiles or target advertising. See Google Fonts privacy and data collection.
  • jsDelivr serves the Three.js library that draws the live 3D robots. See the jsDelivr privacy policy. If your browser asks for reduced motion or reduced data use, or cannot run WebGL, the robots stay as still images and this request is never made.

The contact form

The form on the home page does not send or store anything. When you press “Open in my email app”, it opens a new email in your own mail app, addressed to samirsen66@gmail.com, with your name, email address and message filled in. Nothing is sent until you send that email yourself, and you can edit or discard it first.

The “Copy address” button only writes the email address to your clipboard.

Email you send

If you send an email, Samir receives it in Gmail, so Google also processes it under the Google Privacy Policy. Samir uses your name, address and message only to read and reply to you.

To confirm How long Samir keeps these emails, and whether he deletes them on request.

Links to other sites

Links to GitHub, LinkedIn and other sites take you away from this site. Their own privacy policies apply there.

Changes

If the site starts collecting anything new, this page will say so and its date will change.
